Learn Digital Twins with Phoebe

A warehouse table records what already happened. A digital twin makes a promise about the present: this model describes this particular asset as it is today, it is kept in step with data from it, and someone will decide with it. The promise is only worth the evidence that it still holds. Kittiwake Hall, a constructed six-storey teaching building whose facilities team builds a thermal twin of each floor to plan heating, runs through every session: a twin built from the drawings is 4.45 C off the true room and estimates a week's heat 32.9 percent low; calibrated and kept in sync it is 0.218 C off and its 95 percent band holds 96.8 percent of readings; told its sensors are perfect, the band holds 77.4 percent. Six leader sessions on the decision, ten practitioner sessions on the build. The research and standards are cited; every Kittiwake number is constructed and computed in the page. Nothing here is investment advice or a view on any company.

If you come from a data team

Leader session 6 in three columns. What a conventional analyst, engineer or scientist already has that is worth more here, what is new, and what is actively dangerous.

Carries over

  • Schemas, keys and versioning, now for assets, models and every prediction
  • Residual analysis: reading minus prediction, sliced by floor and by week
  • Train and test discipline, now a calibration week and a different check week
  • Lineage: which model, fitted to which configuration, said this

Must learn

  • State versus parameters: what the asset is doing now versus how it behaves
  • Physics-structured models with few parameters, and which ones the data can pin down
  • Data assimilation: the Kalman predict and update, and what the gain means
  • Uncertainty bands that are checked against what then happened

Must unlearn

  • "The model is done once it is trained": a twin is refitted as the asset changes
  • "Trust the latest reading": at full trust the twin copies the noise, 0.403 C off
  • "Narrower is better": an overconfident band held 77.4 percent, not 95
  • "Config is not data": an unlogged retrofit made one floor's plan 63.1 percent wrong
